Title: Frenemies

Pitch (verbatim): Frenemies: Twitch chat splits into two teams and wages live battles — and when one side dwindles, the other side gains the advantage, just like in a real war.

## Description

Frenemies turns Twitch chat into a Red-versus-Blue battle. Viewers choose with !red or !blue, then vote to attack, defend, heal or charge a surge. Every two seconds, each team's top command executes. More active participants means more attack and healing power, with transparent caps and no hidden catch-up bonuses. A battle ends at zero line strength or after three minutes.

The streamer hosts through a private control page and adds a read-only overlay to OBS. Follow, subscription, raid and Hype Train notifications supply visible team effects. Supply drop, fog and freeze affect the battlefield symmetrically. The kill-switch pauses votes and clears the overlay. Session scores and persistent season wins show who holds the advantage across battles.

Active participants are counted for the whole current battle; inactivity does not remove them. Viewers need no separate installation or game account. They participate through their existing Twitch chat account.

Codex built the server, Twitch integration, overlay, controls, tests, persistence and deployment configuration. It also checked the official rules and exercised a labelled simulated battle in the browser. **Real Twitch verification and deployment are pending; revise this paragraph only after they pass.**
