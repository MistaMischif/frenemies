Your channel experience

View public shop

Share the shop link with chat. Players redeem and equip through their own Twitch identity in chat. Faction labels are customizable; !human / !zombie remain stable chat commands. Currency is earned only, channel-specific, and has no cash value. No Bits exchange or payouts.

Character skins

Upload original or licensed PNG art. Static full-character skins, transparent background recommended. The picker fits art to 512 pixels, preserving its proportions. Items start as drafts. Published items can be retired; existing owners keep them.

Missions and completion stories

Define a mission using supported objectives. Each target counts eligible battles, not repeated messages. A published mission pays once per player. Objectives and rewards are immutable; retiring and republishing never resets completion.

Economy rules

10 currency per eligible battle, +5 for a victory, +20 for the first eligible battle each UTC day. Eligibility: at least two accepted command votes, a battle lasting at least ten seconds, and participants on both teams. The first ten eligible battles per UTC day earn currency and mission progress. Joining, paid effects and repeated commands do not generate currency. No missed-day penalty.

Simulated player test

Demo only. Join a named character, start a battle in controls, then return here to redeem and equip. Demo characters vote automatically and rewards are temporary.

Participation and retention

Measures eligible game players, not everyone watching. D1/D7 mean playing again on the exact UTC calendar day after the first eligible battle. Unmatured cohorts are excluded. Simulation is never mixed with a live channel.

Rolling 90-day daily history; cohort eligibility restricted to the last 80 days. Event counts currently cover supported raid, Hype-begin and subscription/gift campaign events. Connected-chat hours measure observation time, not verified live broadcast hours. No individual watch-time claims.
