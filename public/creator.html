Community episodes

Both factions contribute to a shared objective while competing in battles. Each eligible player contributes at most once per battle. Publish an opening, a success ending and a setback ending. The active episode keeps its own revision when you edit the template. A successful episode can unlock a published skin for everyone who participated in an eligible battle during that episode. Episodes do not mint currency.

Missions and completion stories

Define a mission using supported objectives. Each target counts eligible battles, not repeated messages. A published mission pays once per player. Edit draft rules before publishing. Published names and stories remain editable; duplicate a mission to make a new edition with new rules. Up to 12 live missions and 200 archived/draft editions. Retiring and republishing never resets completion.

Economy rules

10 currency per eligible battle, +5 for a victory, +20 for the first eligible battle each UTC day. Eligibility: your choice resolves at least twice (or two accepted votes) and both factions participate. Your choice repeats every six seconds for this battle; choose again next battle. The first ten eligible battles per UTC day earn base currency; mission progress and one-time mission rewards continue afterward. Showcase ranks cost 50 × the next rank (up to 10) and add an on-stream cosmetic ring, never combat power. Joining, paid effects and repeated commands do not generate currency. No missed-day penalty.

Participation and retention

Measures eligible game players, not everyone watching. D1/D7 mean playing again on the exact UTC calendar day after the first eligible battle. Unmatured cohorts are excluded. Simulation is never mixed with a live channel.

Rolling 90-day daily history; cohort eligibility restricted to the last 80 days. Event counts currently cover supported raid, Hype-begin and subscription/gift campaign events. Connected-chat hours measure observation time, not verified live broadcast hours. No individual watch-time claims.
