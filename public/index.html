Free chat participation. No viewer download or separate account. Optional Bits Barrage depends on Twitch approval and channel eligibility; the game works without it.

01 / JOIN

One command.

Type !join to return to your character's faction or join the smaller roster. Choose explicitly with !human or !zombie. Then !attack, !defend, !heal or !surge.

02 / BATTLE

Make your move.

Choose a tactic once and it repeats every six seconds for this battle. Change it when the fight changes. Both teams have equal action strength, however large the crowd. Battles last up to three minutes.

03 / RETURN

Your story stays.

Earn currency, collect creator-designed skins and unlock community rewards through shared episodes. Inventory persists. Your next broadcast gives the story somewhere to go.

Fits your broadcast.

Connect Twitch, copy one OBS browser-source link, and start a battle. Choose a full arena for a community segment or a compact strip alongside your main content. Use automatic scheduling with intermissions, or start battles yourself. Pause or clear the overlay whenever you need to. Record broadcast samples to measure participation, return visits and contribution after costs.

The demo uses simulated players. It sends no Twitch messages and does not change live season scores.
