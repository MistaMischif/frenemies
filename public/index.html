Turn your community into rival factions. Battle together, survive infection, and return for the next chapter of your channel's story.

Connect your Twitch channel
Open streamer controls

Free chat participation. No viewer download or separate account. Optional Bits Barrage depends on Twitch approval and channel eligibility; the game works without it.

01 / JOIN

One command.

Type !join to return to your character's faction or join the smaller roster. Choose explicitly with !human or !zombie. Then !attack, !defend, !heal or !surge.

02 / BATTLE

Make your move.

Each viewer gets one vote every two seconds. Your faction's winning choice becomes an animated action. Battles last up to three minutes.

03 / RETURN

Your story stays.

Characters earn experience through chapters. Rescue infected Humans, protect the refuge, and meet the convoy when a raid arrives. Live-channel campaign progress persists.

Fits your broadcast.

Connect Twitch, copy one OBS browser-source link, and start a battle. Choose a full arena for a community segment or a compact strip alongside your main content. Pause or clear the overlay whenever you need to.

The demo uses simulated players. It sends no Twitch messages and does not change live season scores.
